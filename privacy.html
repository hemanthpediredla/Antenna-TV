Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Antenna TV is the name under which this app is published. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: I do not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising in Antenna.

What stays on your device

Your playlist details (server address, username and password, M3U link or portal address and MAC address), favourites, watch history, viewing statistics, layout preferences and settings are stored only on your device. Passwords and playlist credentials are kept in the iOS Keychain. This information is never sent to me.

Your IPTV provider

Antenna connects directly from your device to the provider you add, to load channel lists, guides and streams. Your provider receives your device's network address and the credentials you entered, as with any player. I do not operate, control or receive anything from your provider. Their privacy policy applies to that connection.

Ratings (optional)

If you enter your own OMDb API key in Settings, Antenna sends movie and show titles and your key to OMDb (omdbapi.com) to look up IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings. Nothing else is sent. If you leave the key empty, no ratings requests are made.

Purchases

Antenna Pro purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I receive no payment details, name or email from Apple through the app. Apple's privacy policy governs purchases.

Children

Antenna is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Deleting your data

Remove a playlist or clear history and favourites in Settings, or delete the app, to remove all data stored by Antenna from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
