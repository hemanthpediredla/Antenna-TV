Bring the playlist you already have.

Antenna TV works with the three ways providers usually hand you a playlist. Add one in under a minute.

Xtream Codes
Enter the server address, username and password from your provider.
M3U
Paste the playlist link, or import the file from your phone. A guide link is picked up automatically.
Stalker portal
Enter the portal address and your device’s MAC address, as your provider gave them to you.

Built for watching, not for managing.

Live TV

Channels sorted into your provider’s groups, with a programme guide and search. The app reopens on the channel you left, muted until you say otherwise.

Movies and shows

Poster-first rows for every group, seasons and episodes, and Continue Watching. Add your own free OMDb key to see IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Playlists, favourites and history stay on your device, and passwords sit in the iOS Keychain.

Questions people ask.

Does Antenna TV include any channels?

No. It’s a player. You need a playlist from a provider you’re entitled to use.

Will Pro work on my new phone or iPad?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Playlists aren’t synced between devices, so add them again.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your device. There’s no Antenna TV account and nothing is sent to us. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Something won’t play. What should I check?

Make sure your subscription with the provider is active and the playlist details are right, then try another channel. If it still fails, get in touch.
