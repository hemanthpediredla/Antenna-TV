Support

Support for Antenna TV is provided by Hemanth Pediredla. Email hemanth.pediredla96@gmail.com and include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. I aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where do I get channels?

Antenna does not provide content. Add the playlist from your own IPTV provider: Xtream Codes login, an M3U link or file, or a Stalker portal address and MAC address.

A channel or video won't play

Check that your subscription with the provider is active and that the playlist details are correct. Try another channel, then remove and re-add the playlist if needed.

Restoring Pro on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Antenna and tap Restore Purchases. Playlists are not synced between devices, so add them again.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Antenna.
